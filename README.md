# The World in One Day — 21 November 2026

My entry for [Sum up 21 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261121) on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) draws a newspaper front page for
Saturday, 21 November 2026 — the day's news, drawn from the reporting of
Reuters, the Associated Press and CNN — and writes one 1024×1024 PNG to
`/output/entry.png`. No network, no randomness: two runs write the same bytes.

## The day's stories

- **Lead — Iran strikes Bahrain.** Iran's Revolutionary Guards say missiles
  and drones hit US positions at Al-Riffa and Awali; Bahrain says the attack
  hit a civilian industrial site, starting a fire, with no injuries.
- **Mexico — "Chapo Isidro" captured.** Mexican forces seize FBI most-wanted
  Fausto Isidro Meza Flores in Sinaloa; at least 11 dead as gunfights follow.
- **Ukraine — missile hits Kryvyi Rih.** A triple-tap strike kills a boy, 7,
  and a paramedic; 28 of about 60 wounded remain in hospital.
- **Baltic — ship ablaze in the Gulf of Finland.** Drones hit the
  military-linked Sparta II; its 16 crew escape by lifeboat.
- **Climate — COP31 close to a deal.** The Antalya talks run past deadline;
  the summit chief says a text all parties can agree on is ready.
- **Markets — Nasdaq erases its 2026 gains.** S&P 500 −3.7%, Dow −1.9%,
  Nasdaq −5.3%, Nvidia −14%.
- **Also today:** Wolbachia mosquitoes cut child dengue cases 69% in a Brazil
  study; Trump defends his Putin ties, Zelensky calls Putin "a killer";
  Nvidia halts H200 chip exports to China; Nepal's PM begins his first India
  visit.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
